## The story behind the book

Imagine arriving in London with two flats to choose from. One is cheaper. The other would get you home in time to have dinner with your family. A friend says, “Speak to Mohamed. He can help you compare the rent with the journey you’ll make every day.” In one sentence, you understand why an introduction might be worth your time.

I started working remotely in 2005. By 2010 I was helping newcomers find homes in London. Living in several countries taught me how much can get lost when an introduction crosses a border. A familiar job title may need explaining. A concrete example of helping someone make a decision usually travels further.

That is the idea behind *Global Personal Branding: A Practical Guide to Building Your International Presence*. Try this exercise with someone outside your profession. Send them the first line of your bio. Ask, “Who would you introduce me to, and what would you say I could help them do?” Listen for the detail they remember. It may deserve a place in your opening line.
