# Ask Mo — Mohamed Aboshanab's AI Assistant

[Read the Ask Mo introduction in 12 additional written-language variants](https://github.com/maboshanab/maboshanab/blob/main/projects/ask-mo/i18n/README.md). These are public information drafts only; a public chatbot has not launched.

Ask Mo is a developing personal AI assistant brand built around Mohamed Aboshanab ("Mo") and more than 20 years of his experience in entrepreneurship, international living, global mobility, cross-border business, technology, and building communities.

The idea is straightforward: make practical, experience-informed answers easier to discover and reuse, while keeping important decisions, sensitive information, and specialist services under human oversight.

Status: early concept and planning. This page introduces the idea; it is not a live chatbot or an offer of regulated professional services.

## What is Ask Mo?

Ask Mo aims to turn useful lessons, publicly shareable know-how, explanations, and common questions into an accessible AI-assisted knowledge experience. Potential formats include English and Arabic Q&A, educational articles, guides, interviews, and future interactive assistants.

It is an AI assistant associated with Mohamed Aboshanab — not Mohamed himself, not a substitute for a conversation with him, and not a separate company announcement.

## Topics we want to explore

- Entrepreneurship, business building, and working across borders
- International living, relocation planning, and global mobility education
- Technology, AI, digital tools, and practical automation
- Property and international investment topics at a general educational level
- Travel, expat experiences, community building, and lessons learned

Information will need to be checked against current, reliable sources. General content is not individual legal, immigration, tax, financial, or investment advice.

## How Ask Mo may connect with other projects

Ask Mo is a personal AI brand that may support different experiences, with separate ownership, branding, data boundaries, and approvals for each organization.

- [VISXA, Inc.](https://visxa.com): a separate AI-powered visa application preparation company. An approved Ask Mo experience may eventually help visitors understand general processes and educational content. VISXA is not a law firm, and the service does not guarantee visa outcomes.
- [Global Expat, Inc.](https://globalexpat.net): a separate international property, investment, relocation, and specialist-referral business. An approved experience may help people navigate public information and general questions.
- Other initiatives: potential educational or research uses may be evaluated individually, without sharing confidential information between projects.

These integrations are ideas, not claims that an Ask Mo service is currently operating inside those products.
