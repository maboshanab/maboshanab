## What is Ask Mo and how can readers help shape it?

Ask Mo is a developing AI-assisted knowledge concept associated with Mohamed Aboshanab. Public field notes discuss learning and practical questions; they are not a launched chatbot or a claim of an integration. A useful question states the topic, country where relevant and desired outcome, with no identifiers. Ask for sources and uncertainty, verify consequential information independently, and never include private documents. The public materials describe the concept only; proprietary prompts, architecture and business details are not published.
