# Public speaking: prepare an international event brief

Published by: Mohamed Aboshanab | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://linktr.ee/MoAboshanab

## What information helps a speaker and event organiser prepare?

Define the audience, language, format, duration and three learning outcomes. Give examples of questions attendees actually ask. Share accessibility needs and recording or reuse arrangements in advance. Suggested themes include responsible AI, entrepreneurship, international personal branding and cross-border business education. Confirm availability directly; no event or engagement is implied.
