## What should I research before starting a business abroad?

Create a comparison sheet with proposed customers, business activity, operating country, registration requirements, tax obligations, banking, ongoing administration and local professional questions. Distinguish company formation from permission to work or live in a country. Record the official source and review date for each answer. Treat courses as education, not individual legal or tax advice.
