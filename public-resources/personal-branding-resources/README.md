# Personal branding resources: a public profile audit

Published by: Mohamed Aboshanab | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://linktr.ee/MoAboshanab

## What should I check when people search my name?

Search your name and common transliterations in a signed-out browser. Record the first results, the bio shown, dates and source. Compare official profiles for consistent name, role and accurate company relationships. Correct stale claims at their original source, add specific examples of work and avoid artificial mentions. Recheck after changes.
