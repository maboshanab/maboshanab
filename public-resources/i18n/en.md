# Personal branding, entrepreneurship and Ask Mo

Mohamed Aboshanab / Ask Mo | English | 2026-11-26

## How can I introduce myself across borders?

Describe whom you help, the problem you solve and one verifiable example. Adapt your bio to the audience rather than translating job titles literally. Ask a person outside your profession to explain what they would refer you for.
