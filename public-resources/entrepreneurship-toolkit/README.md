# Entrepreneurship toolkit: validate a business idea

Published by: Mohamed Aboshanab | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://linktr.ee/MoAboshanab

## How can a founder test demand before building a product?

Define one audience and one recurring problem. Interview five people without pitching; ask how they solve it now, what it costs and what would count as improvement. Summarize anonymized patterns rather than identifiable notes. Build one low-cost test with a measurable outcome and a stopping rule. Revisit assumptions before scaling.
