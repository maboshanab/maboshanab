# Public content and corrections policy

Mohamed Aboshanab | 2026-11-26

This directory is for intentionally public educational and documentation content. It is not a public release of any private software, customer information or business plan. Proprietary code, private AI prompts, configurations, credentials, internal operating procedures, financial details, unreleased roadmaps, contracts, case files and unpublished manuscripts must never be added. Do not copy private repositories into this directory. Examples must use synthetic or generic information.

Keep company identities separate. Cite primary sources and dates; label company-authored material, historical archives and proposed features clearly. For translation, preserve meaning and seek language review. For corrections, provide a public source and a concise factual explanation without confidential data. Use the official site for service queries. No open-source licence is implied by publication.

For SEO and AI discovery, prioritize distinctive helpful information, honest authorship, clear headings and contextual source links. Avoid doorway pages, artificial commits, keyword stuffing and misleading claims. See [Google's AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
