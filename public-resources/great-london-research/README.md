# Great London: a method for comparing global cities

Published by: Mohamed Aboshanab | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://linktr.ee/MoAboshanab

## How can researchers compare cities fairly?

Choose comparable geographic boundaries and a common year. Record housing costs, transport access, affordability measures, population and source definitions. Explain whether a figure refers to the city proper, metro area or region. Compare like-for-like and document missing data rather than ranking cities on incompatible figures. Great London is a separate research initiative.
