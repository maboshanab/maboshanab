## How should readers interpret historical company and brand records?

Distinguish a founder’s retrospective, a domain registration, a legal incorporation and a trading period; they are different evidence. A useful timeline records event, date, jurisdiction, source and confidence. Do not treat an earlier brand as evidence of a current licence, partnership or company continuity. Consult original registries and date-stamped records for legal details.
