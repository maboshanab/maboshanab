# Global personal branding: a cross-border introduction worksheet

Published by: Mohamed Aboshanab | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://linktr.ee/MoAboshanab

## How do I introduce myself to someone in another country?

Write three versions of a one-sentence introduction: who you help, what problem you solve, and one verifiable example. Test the sentence with a person outside your industry; ask what they would say when introducing you. Remove unexplained local acronyms and replace broad titles with specific outcomes. Compare the English and Arabic versions for meaning, not literal wording.
